# **App Name**: ConferVerse

## Core Features:

- AI Proposal Mock Scoring: Simulate an AI scoring system that rates submitted proposals on relevance, clarity, and technical depth; this 'tool' will generate scores and provide constructive feedback, allowing organizers to assess and refine proposals efficiently.
- Drag-and-Drop Agenda Builder: Enable event organizers to construct the event agenda via drag-and-drop in a dashboard environment. Proposed sessions are displayed in a timeline format, while any potential scheduling imbalances are highlighted to optimize session distribution and event flow.
- Automated Personalized Communication: Automatically draft emails for session acceptance or rejection with an AI 'tool' based on templates. Dynamic content such as speaker name, session title, and personalized feedback is added to increase engagement.
- Speaker Dashboard: Speakers can log in to monitor the status of their session proposals, upload presentation documents, confirm their availability, and access relevant resources and templates; it features a progress bar to confirm steps for the speakers.
- QR Code Generator for Event Tools: Generate individual QR codes for smooth speaker check-ins and T-shirt collection. Speakers' check-in can be easily marked complete by toggling a digital switch that marks the QR as 'scanned'.
- Session Title Suggestions: Utilize AI to refine session titles. Propose 2-3 variations for titles when speakers would like fresh ideas or ways to increase engagement.
- Session Feedback Collection: Capture session-specific speaker ratings and attendee comments that are visible within each speaker session, to drive future conference and speaker improvement.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated teal (#008080) to evoke professionalism and trust, relating to speaking and authority.
- Background color: Light grayish-teal (#E0EEEE), a very desaturated version of the primary color, to provide a gentle, unobtrusive backdrop.
- Accent color: Soft cyan (#70D6D0), an analogous color that is less saturated and lighter than the primary, to draw attention to important elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a grotesque sans-serif with a modern, neutral, machined look suitable for both headlines and body.
- Use minimalist, professional icons for navigation and key actions. Icons should use the accent color.
- Mobile-first layout, leveraging Tailwind CSS grid and flexbox for responsive design. Use cards and progress bars for clear content organization.
- Subtle transitions and animations for user interactions, such as expanding session details or loading AI suggestions.
